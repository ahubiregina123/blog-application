ABOUT

A Publication for

careful readers.

Longform was founded in 2024 with a simple premise: there is still an appetite for writing that asks something of the reader. We publish essays on design, craft, technology and the slow art of paying attention.

We are not interested in content. We are interested in ideas - formed slowly, expressed precisely and allowed to sit with you for a while.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Depth over frequency

We publish when a piece is ready, not when the calendar says it is time. Every essay is edited for clarity, argument and rhythm.

No engagement traps

No comment counts, clap buttons, or social metric. reading is enough. We trust you to decide what is worth returning to.

Long-term thinking

We write about things that will still matter in ten years. Trend pieces and new commmentary are a form of pollution we choose not to produce.

Want to contribute?

We read every submission. We reply to ones we think have something to say.
